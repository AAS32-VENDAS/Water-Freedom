How Atmospheric Water Generation Works

Air contains varying amounts of water vapor. When humid air is cooled below its dew point, some of that vapor condenses into liquid water—similar to droplets forming on a cold glass.

A working system also needs suitable components, energy, collection and storage, and appropriate treatment. Actual output depends on humidity, temperature, design, and operating conditions.
